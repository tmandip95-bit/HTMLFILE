About menu

Welcome to our food menu ,where every dish is boldly delicious and frightfully fun.

Our cooked chicken is prepared with care to ensure every bite is tender, juicy, and full of flavor. We use only the finest cuts, marinated with a blend of aromatic spices and herbs, then cooked to perfection to lock in natural juices. The result is a rich, satisfying dish that’s both hearty and wholesome, perfect on its own or paired with rice, bread, or fresh vegetables. Ideal for family meals, casual dining, or special occasions, our cooked chicken delivers comfort, taste, and quality in every serving.

Tender pieces of slow-cooked mutton simmered in a rich onion–tomato gravy, infused with aromatic spices and traditional herbs. Deeply flavorful, hearty, and perfectly balanced, this classic dish pairs beautifully with steamed rice, naan, or roti.

A comforting Himalayan noodle soup, Thukpa features soft noodles in a savory broth, tossed with fresh vegetables and tender pieces of meat or served vegetarian. Warm, aromatic, and wholesome, it’s the perfect dish to soothe the soul and satisfy the appetite.
